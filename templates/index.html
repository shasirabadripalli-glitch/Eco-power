Hardware Prototype Specifications & Wiring

Full Specs Page

ESP32 Microcontroller Node

Built-in 2.4 GHz WiFi, dual-core processing, local ADC light sensing, and 3-channel optocoupler relay control for priority loads.

INA219 High-Precision Power Sensor

Measures DC voltage (0–26V) and current up to $\pm3.2\text{A}$ over I2C bus (`GPIO 21 SDA / GPIO 22 SCL`) for accurate $P = V \times I$ calculation.

3-Channel Relay Module

Channel 1 (GPIO 25): High Priority Essential Load.
Channel 2 (GPIO 26): Medium Priority Fan.
Channel 3 (GPIO 27): Low Priority Heater.

Low-Voltage Prototype Safety

Operates strictly on safe 12V DC power with isolated components. Safe for classroom, laboratory, and hackathon presentation environments.
